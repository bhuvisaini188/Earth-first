What Can You Do? 🌱

Protecting our planet doesn't always require huge changes. Everyday choices and collective action can make a difference.

Small Actions. Big Impact.

Climate change is a global challenge, but that doesn't mean we are powerless. From changing our daily habits to speaking up for the environment, there are many ways we can contribute to a healthier planet.

👕

1. Reuse and Repurpose Clothes

Instead of throwing away clothes you no longer wear, donate them, exchange them with others or turn old fabric into useful items such as tote bags, cushion covers and cleaning cloths.

Try this: Organise a clothes collection or exchange drive in your school or community.
🌱

2. Turn Food Waste into Fertiliser

Fruit and vegetable scraps can be composted to create nutrient-rich material for soil. Compost can be used in gardens and plant beds instead of sending food scraps to landfill.

Try this: Start a composting project at home or school with suitable food scraps.
💧

3. Save Electricity and Water

Switch off lights, fans and appliances when they aren't needed. Turn off taps properly, avoid unnecessary water use and report leaking taps.

Try this: Make it a habit to check lights, fans and taps before leaving a room.
🚌

4. Choose Public Transport

Cars and other vehicles release greenhouse gases. Using buses, trains or shared transport can reduce emissions per passenger. Walking and cycling are also good options for shorter journeys when safe.

Try this: Choose a shared journey or public transport whenever practical.
📢

5. Speak Up for the Environment

Raise awareness about issues such as deforestation, pollution and habitat destruction. Share reliable information, organise awareness campaigns and participate in peaceful environmental events.

Try this: Create an awareness campaign or poster about protecting forests.
🌳

6. Plant and Protect Trees

Trees absorb carbon dioxide and provide habitats for wildlife. Participate in local tree-planting activities using suitable native species, and remember that caring for existing trees is just as important.

Try this: Join a local tree-care activity and help look after newly planted trees.

The Future Starts With Us 🌍

No single person can solve climate change alone. But when individuals, schools and communities work together, small actions can contribute to meaningful change.

Don't wait for someone else to make a difference. Start today.
