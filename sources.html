Sources & References 📚

Reliable information is important when understanding climate change. Explore the organisations and resources that provide scientific information about our planet.

Where Does Our Information Come From?

The following organisations publish scientific research, environmental information and educational resources. They are useful starting points for learning more about climate change and its effects.

1. NASA Climate Change

NASA provides scientific information about global warming, rising temperatures, melting ice, sea-level rise and other evidence of climate change.

Visit NASA Climate →

2. United Nations

The United Nations explains the causes and effects of climate change and discusses international efforts to address the climate crisis.

Visit UN Climate Action →

3. IPCC

The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change assesses scientific research and publishes reports about climate change, its impacts and possible solutions.

Visit IPCC →

4. World Health Organization

The WHO provides information about how climate change affects human health, including heat-related risks, air quality and the spread of certain diseases.

Visit WHO →

5. UN Environment Programme

UNEP covers environmental issues such as pollution, waste, deforestation, biodiversity and actions that can help protect the environment.

Visit UNEP →

6. World Meteorological Organization

The WMO provides information about global weather, climate patterns, extreme weather events and climate monitoring.

Visit WMO →

About the Use of AI

AI-generated information can contain mistakes, so important scientific facts should always be checked against reliable sources.
