02 — UNDERSTANDING THE IMPACTS

What Changes Around the World?

Although climate impacts differ between regions, several environmental changes are being observed across the planet.

🔥

Extreme Heat

Rising temperatures increase the likelihood and intensity of many heat extremes.

🧊

Melting Glaciers

Glaciers are losing ice in many mountain regions, affecting water systems and contributing to sea-level rise.

🌊

Rising Sea Levels

Rising seas increase coastal flooding and erosion risks for many communities.

🌳

Changing Ecosystems

Changing temperatures and rainfall can affect habitats, biodiversity and wildlife.

🌧️

Changing Rainfall

Some areas face increasing heavy rainfall, while others experience greater drought risks.

🌾

Food Security

Climate impacts can affect food production, farming livelihoods and food availability.

03 — A GLOBAL CHALLENGE

Why Are Some Regions More Vulnerable?

Not every country has the same resources to prepare for or respond to climate change. Vulnerability depends on several factors.

🏘️

Location

Coastal areas, dry regions and places exposed to extreme weather can face particular risks.

💧

Natural Resources

Communities that depend heavily on rainfall, farming or natural ecosystems can be more exposed to climate changes.

🏥

Infrastructure

Access to healthcare, safe housing, transport and early-warning systems can influence how communities respond.

🤝

Resources and Support

Financial resources, technology and planning help communities prepare for climate impacts.

One important fact

Climate change is a global issue, but its impacts are not distributed equally. Some communities experience greater risks despite contributing relatively little to historical greenhouse gas emissions.
