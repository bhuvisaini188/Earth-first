THE SCIENCE BEHIND OUR CHANGING PLANET

Understanding Climate Change

What is happening to our planet? Why is it happening? And why does it matter? Let's explore the science behind one of the biggest challenges facing humanity.

01 — THE BASICS

What is climate change?

Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth's temperatures and weather patterns. These changes can happen naturally, but human activities have become the main driver of the current rapid warming of our planet.

Since the Industrial Revolution, activities such as burning fossil fuels, deforestation and industrial production have increased the amount of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.

Did you know?

Climate change is not just about rising temperatures. It can also affect rainfall, oceans, ecosystems, extreme weather patterns and the availability of food and water.
02 — KNOW THE DIFFERENCE

Weather vs Climate

🌧️

Weather

Weather describes the conditions of the atmosphere over a short period of time, such as hours or days.

Example: It is raining today.

🌍

Climate

Climate describes the average weather conditions of a region over a long period, usually 30 years or more.

Example: A region usually has hot, dry summers.

03 — THE CAUSES

What causes climate change?

Several human activities release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere. These gases trap additional heat and contribute to global warming.

🏭

Burning Fossil Fuels

Coal, oil and natural gas are burned to produce electricity, power vehicles and run industries. This releases carbon dioxide.

🌳

Deforestation

Trees absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. Cutting down forests reduces this ability and can release stored carbon.

🚗

Transportation

Cars, trucks, ships and aeroplanes often burn fossil fuels, releasing greenhouse gases.

🌾

Agriculture

Farming activities, livestock and fertiliser use release greenhouse gases such as methane and nitrous oxide.

04 — THE SCIENCE

The Greenhouse Effect

The greenhouse effect is a natural process that keeps Earth warm enough to support life. However, human activities have strengthened this effect by increasing greenhouse gas concentrations.

1

Sunlight reaches Earth

Energy from the Sun travels through the atmosphere and warms Earth's surface.

2

Earth releases heat

Earth's surface releases energy back towards space as infrared radiation.

3

Greenhouse gases absorb heat

Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit some of this infrared radiation, warming the atmosphere.

4

More greenhouse gases, more warming

Human activities increase greenhouse gas concentrations, strengthening the warming effect.

Important greenhouse gases

Carbon dioxide (CO₂): Mainly from fossil fuel burning and deforestation.

Methane (CH₄): From livestock, landfills and fossil fuel production.

Nitrous oxide (N₂O): From agricultural activities and other sources.

05 — THE CONSEQUENCES

How does it affect our planet?

Climate change affects natural environments, wildlife and human communities around the world.

🧊

Melting Ice

Rising temperatures cause glaciers and ice sheets to lose ice, contributing to rising sea levels.

🌊

Rising Sea Levels

Sea levels rise as ocean water expands when it warms and land-based ice melts.

🔥

Heatwaves

Many regions are experiencing more frequent and intense extreme heat.

🐾

Wildlife and Ecosystems

Changing temperatures and habitats can make survival more difficult for many species.

🌧️

Changing Rainfall

Rainfall patterns are changing, increasing the risk of droughts in some places and heavy rainfall in others.

🌽

Food and Water

Changes in temperature and rainfall can affect crop production and water availability.

06 — SEPARATING FACT FROM FICTION

Climate Change: Myths vs Facts

MYTH: Climate change is just about hotter weather.

FACT: It affects rainfall, oceans, ecosystems and weather patterns too.

MYTH: One cold day disproves global warming.

FACT: Weather changes daily, while climate describes long-term patterns.

MYTH: Climate change is entirely natural.

FACT: Natural factors affect climate, but human activities are the main cause of current global warming.

MYTH: Individual actions make no difference.

FACT: Individual choices, combined with community action and government policies, can contribute to meaningful change.

07 — BE PART OF THE SOLUTION

What can we do?

Tackling climate change requires action from individuals, communities, businesses and governments. Everyone has a role to play.

♻️

Reduce Waste

Reuse materials, recycle correctly and avoid unnecessary consumption.

💡

Save Energy

Use energy efficiently and support renewable energy sources.

🌱

Protect Nature

Protect forests, restore ecosystems and support biodiversity.

📢

Spread Awareness

Share reliable information and encourage others to take climate action.
