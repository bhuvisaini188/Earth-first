02 — LOOKING AHEAD

What might the future look like?

Climate change happens over decades. Here are two important time horizons scientists use when studying possible future conditions.

2050

By the middle of the century, choices about energy, transportation, land use and emissions will have influenced the climate. Communities will also need to adapt to changes already underway.

2100

By the end of the century, differences between lower-emissions and higher-emissions pathways could become much more significant, affecting ecosystems, oceans and human communities.

03 — AN IMPORTANT REMINDER

Is the future already decided?

No. These scenarios are not predictions of exactly what will happen. They are ways scientists explore possible outcomes under different assumptions about future greenhouse gas emissions.

Our choices matter.

Reducing emissions can limit future warming. Preparing communities and protecting nature can also reduce the risks and impacts of climate change. The future is influenced by decisions made today.

Be Part of the Future

The future of Earth isn't just something we wait for. It is something we help shape through the choices we make, the policies we support and the actions we take together.
